# Benchmarking Guide

Protocols for characterizing room-temperature qubit performance.

## Randomized Benchmarking (RB)

Standard Clifford RB per Knill et al. (2008). Extracts average gate error rate.

## Cross-Entropy Benchmarking (XEB)

Google-style XEB per Arute et al. (2019). Estimates computational fidelity.

## Quantum Process Tomography (QPT)

Full process matrix reconstruction for single and two-qubit gates.
